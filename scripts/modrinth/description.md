# IndustrialCraft 2 Community Edition

**IC2 Community Edition** is an unofficial, community-made port of
[IndustrialCraft 2](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/industrial-craft) (`2.8.222-ex112`,
Minecraft 1.12.2 / Forge) to **Minecraft 1.21.1 / Fabric**.

The goal is to keep classic IC2 playable on modern Minecraft: the same EU energy net, machines,
generators, cables, tools, armor, crops and UU-matter, with as little reinvention as possible.

> ⚠️ **Alpha.** Most content is ported, but expect bugs and missing features. Back up your worlds.

## Features

- EU energy net with cables, transformers and storage blocks
- Processing machines: macerator, extractor, compressor, electric furnace, ore washer, metal former and more
- Generators: fuel, geothermal, solar, wind, water, kinetic and steam
- Electric tools and armor, including jetpacks and the nano/quantum suits
- IC2 crops: crop sticks, cross-breeding, Cropnalyzer and Crop Harvester
- UU-matter, scrap and the mass fabricator
- Rubber trees and ores in world generation
- Uses vanilla copper and works with other mods' metals through common tags
- Optional [Roughly Enough Items](https://modrinth.com/mod/rei) integration with recipe transfer

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- Fabric Loader 0.16.0 or newer
- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
- Java 21

## Reporting issues

This is **not** the official IndustrialCraft 2. Please report problems with this port on our
[issue tracker](https://github.com/faustyu1/ic2/issues), not to the original IC2 team.
